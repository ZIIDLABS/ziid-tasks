## You forgot something today. Didn't you?

Notes get buried. Alarms get ignored. Your brain is busy.

ZIID Tasks is a small Android app with one promise: **it remembers for you, and it shows up exactly where you can't miss it, on your lock screen.**

No digging through lists. No unlocking your phone to find out what you forgot. Ziid just appears.

---

## How it works

| 1. Create | 2. Pin | 3. Get woken up |
|---|---|---|
| Tap **+ New Task** and write what you need to do. | Choose the **time** and the **place**. | At the right moment, a **floating reminder** appears on your lock screen. |

---

## Features

- **Floating lock-screen reminders:** the reminder appears right on your lock screen, so you see it without unlocking.
- **Time and place:** every task is tied to when and where you need it.
- **Smart search:** find any task or place instantly from the search bar.
- **Categories:** filter your tasks by All, Study Spot, Gym, Work and more.
- **Streak counter:** a small flame keeps you motivated.
- **Dark mode:** easy on the eyes at night.
- **Light and fast:** about 19 MB, and it runs on any Android phone.
- **Ziid:** a mascot with personality who makes reminders feel friendly instead of annoying.

## FAQ

**Is ZIID Tasks free?**
[ free with no ads]

**Why do I need to allow installing from outside the Play Store?**
This app is distributed as an APK from this repository. Android asks for your confirmation for every app installed this way. You can turn the setting off again after installing.

**Reminders aren't appearing on my lock screen. What should I do?**
Check that notifications are allowed for ZIID Tasks and that battery saving isn't restricting the app in the background. Some phone brands are strict about background apps.
